# Pilot Case Study Requirements

No real case study should be published until it is based on an actual buyer pilot. This file defines the evidence needed to create one without fabricating proof.

## Required Inputs

- Buyer segment and role: defense, aerospace, naval, or advanced manufacturing sourcing team.
- Work package type: drawing, PO line list, controlled RFQ package, or evidence-vault cleanup.
- Baseline workflow: current email, spreadsheet, shared-drive, or ERP process.
- Pilot workflow: intake, classification gate, supplier evidence review, deterministic matching, RFQ invite, data-room access, supplier response, and audit export.
- Measured before/after data:
  - RFQs sent to suppliers missing required evidence.
  - RFQs routed to manual review.
  - Evidence expirations caught before release.
  - Data-room views/downloads logged.
  - Revoked or expired invites.
  - Supplier quote, decline, and no-bid response counts.
- Buyer-approved quote or anonymized summary.
- Permission to publish logo, name, or anonymized case study.

## Safe Case Study Structure

1. Buyer context.
2. Controlled sourcing problem.
3. Pilot scope.
4. Workflow used.
5. Measured operational result.
6. Audit and risk-reduction result.
7. What remained manual.
8. Next pilot expansion.

## Sample Non-Published Draft Skeleton

Headline: Controlled RFQs moved from email release to evidence-gated data-room release.

Problem: The buyer needed to know which suppliers had current evidence before releasing sensitive RFQ files.

Pilot: Sovereign Source was used to structure the work package, check facility-level capability and evidence status, release RFQ invites through data-room access, and export a server-side audit trail.

Result: Fill with measured pilot data only.

Quote: Fill only after written buyer approval.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not imply certification of supplier compliance.
- Do not claim legal export-control determinations.
- Do not use invented logos, customer names, testimonials, or ROI numbers.
- Do not present demo data as buyer production data.
